Our story

It started with a biscuit tin.

Our company was established in 1970 in the name of CV. Harum Manis, when Khong Guan Biscuit first arrived in Indonesia. We were appointed to be a distributor for modern and traditional trade in Jakarta area. Since then, we have become food and snacks distributor.
